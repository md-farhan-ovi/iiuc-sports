Tournament Rules & Guidelines

This rule book contains all the general guidelines, regulations and sports specific rules for the Sports Fest 2026

Only officially registered department students are eligible to participate.

Each team must complete registration before the deadline.

Every player must carry a valid student ID card during the match.

Teams must report to the venue at least 30 minutes before the scheduled match.

Late arrival may result in a walkover decision by the organizing committee.

Fair play and respectful behavior are mandatory for all participants.

Any form of misconduct or violation of the rules may lead to disqualification.

The organizing committee reserves the right to modify the schedule or rules if necessary.
